### 第 2 頁
禪之呼吸~腹式呼吸
As you breathe in, let your belly rise; as you breathe out, let it fall
要領：鼻子進、鼻子吐、氣息要越來越細、柔、綿、緩、長
Inhale gently through your nose, exhale through your nose. Let your breath gradually become finer, softer, more continuous, and longer.
心法：放鬆、自在、跟隨呼吸(隨息法)
Stay relaxed, at ease, and gently let your awareness follow your breath.
